Privacy Policy

Effective with version 1.1.0 (updated 2026-11-26). The previous version of this policy applied to version 1.0.0.

This policy applies to the Moxian app for iPhone ("Moxian: Line Puzzles"), made by XUEKE.

In short: Moxian itself does not collect personal data and has no accounts. Starting with version 1.1.0 the app can show an optional video ad (you choose to watch one to get a hint or an extra heart). Those ads are provided by Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not run servers for the app, and the app sends nothing to us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

When you choose to watch a video ad, and when the app prepares one in the background, the Google Mobile Ads SDK contacts Google. Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses it to show, measure and protect ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

The app shows no banner ads and no ads that interrupt play. If no ad is available, you may still receive the hint or heart.

Tracking and your choices

  • After you have played a few levels, iOS asks whether Moxian may track you. If you allow it, Google may use the advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice later in the app's Settings under "Ad privacy choices".

What stays on your device

Your level progress, hint count, daily-puzzle record and settings are saved on your device so the game can continue where you left off. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device, it may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own Apple Account.

Children

Moxian is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.

Contact
