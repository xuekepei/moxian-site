Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-10

This policy applies to the Moxian app for iPhone ("Moxian: Line Puzzles"), made by XUEKE.

In short: Moxian does not collect, store or share any personal data. The app never connects to the internet.

What the app collects

Nothing. Moxian has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and it makes no network requests.

What stays on your device

Your level progress, hint count, daily-puzzle record and settings are saved on your device so the game can continue where you left off. This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device, it may be included in that backup, which is handled by Apple under your own Apple Account.

Tracking

Moxian does not track you across apps or websites and does not use the advertising identifier.

Children

Moxian is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version changes any of the above, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy: xuekepei@gmail.com
