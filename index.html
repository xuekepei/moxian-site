Moxian · 墨线

Four line puzzles in one quiet app, drawn like ink on paper. For iPhone.

Arrows · 箭阵 · アロー

Tap an arrow and it flies off the board the way it points. Work out the order so that none of them crash.

Connect · 连线 · コネクト

Drag from one dot to its match. Lines can't cross, and the whole board has to be covered.

Loop · 回路 · ループ

Turn the tiles until every line end meets another.

Lights · 点灯 · ライト

Each tap flips a light and the four around it. Turn them all off.

Endless levels in every game, a daily puzzle for each, four color palettes, no timers.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No account. No data collected. Works offline.
