Moxian Support

Thank you for playing Moxian. This page covers the Moxian app for iPhone, made by XUEKE.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and suggestions are welcome by email: xuekepei@gmail.com

It helps to mention your iPhone model, iOS version, the game and level number, and what happened.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get more hints?

You start with three hints, shared by all four games. Finishing a game's daily puzzle earns one more, once per game per day.

I ran out of hearts in Arrows. What now?

The level starts again from the beginning. Levels are short, and the same level number is always the same puzzle.

Is my level the same as everyone else's?

Yes. Levels are generated from the level number, and each day's daily puzzles are the same worldwide.

Does the app need an internet connection?

No. Moxian works fully offline and never connects to the network.

Where is my progress stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device. It is included in your device backup, but there is no separate cloud sync, and it is removed if you delete the app.

How do I change the colors, sound or haptics?

Open Settings from the home screen. All four palettes are free.
